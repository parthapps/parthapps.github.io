Privacy Policy

App: Library Map (Self Study Library & Seat Management)  •  Last updated: 30 October 2026

सार (हिंदी में): Library Map आपका डेटा (छात्रों के नाम, मोबाइल नंबर, फीस रिकॉर्ड) सिर्फ आपके अपने फोन में रखता है। डेवलपर के पास यह डेटा नहीं आता, कोई अकाउंट, विज्ञापन या ट्रैकिंग नहीं है। WhatsApp बटन दबाने पर ही संदेश आपके फोन के WhatsApp में खुलता है।

1. Who we are

Library Map is a free tool for study-library owners to manage seats, students, fee dues and receipts. Developer contact: Parth Sharma, apps.mishraji@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter in the app

You may enter your name, library name, address, mobile number and UPI ID, and your students' names, mobile numbers, seat/shift details and fee payment records.

  • This information is stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage).
  • The developer does not receive, collect, upload, sell or share this information. We do not run any server that stores your library data.
  • There is no sign-up, no login, no advertising and no analytics or tracking in the app.

3. What leaves your device

  • WhatsApp messages. When you tap a WhatsApp button (fee reminder, receipt, share library), the app opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message. The message and the phone number are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you press send in WhatsApp.
  • Phone calls. The "Call" button opens your phone dialer.
  • Backup files. When you tap "Download JSON", a backup file is saved on your device. Where you keep or send that file is your choice and responsibility.
  • App files and notices. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. When it opens, it loads its own files, a notice file (notice.json) and the Google Fonts typeface. These servers (GitHub and Google) may see technical information such as your IP address, as with any website. No library or student data is sent in these requests.

4. Your students' data

You are responsible for the personal details of your students that you store in the app. Please keep only what you need, and keep your phone and backup files safe.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Because the data is only on your device, the developer cannot recover it for you. Please download a backup regularly (Settings → Download JSON).

6. Children

The app is meant for library owners and staff (adults). It is not directed at children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
